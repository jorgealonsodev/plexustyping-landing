---
question: "How do I check that the download is authentic?"
order: 8
doc: empezar/verificar-descarga
lang: en
---

Every version publishes a `SHA256SUMS` file with the sums of all its artifacts. On Linux, with the files in the same folder, run `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`. More in [verify the download](/en/docs/getting-started/verify-download).
