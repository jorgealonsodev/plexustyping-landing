---
question: "Which systems does it run on?"
order: 2
doc: referencia/requisitos-del-sistema
lang: en
---

On Linux x86_64 (the `.deb` package for Ubuntu 22.04 or later, or the `.tar.gz` for any distribution with WebKitGTK 4.1 and GTK 3) and on 64-bit Windows 10 and 11. macOS is not included. See the [system requirements](/en/docs/reference/system-requirements).
