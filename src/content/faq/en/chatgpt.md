question: "Can I use my ChatGPT subscription instead of a key?"
order: 6

lang: en

Yes. The “ChatGPT (tu suscripción)” (your subscription) provider uses OpenAI's official sign-in in the browser and your plan's quota, with no API key. A plan error never falls back to spending your other keys. How to do it in [sign in with ChatGPT](/en/docs/ai/sign-in-with-chatgpt).
