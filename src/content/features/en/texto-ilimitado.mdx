## How the text grows

You start with a text of about 600 characters. When you reach the middle, or when 400 characters or fewer are left ahead, the app asks in the background for a continuation of about 600 characters and appends it after a space, without you stopping. There is always text ahead.

In prose, the AI writes the continuation from the last 400 characters, in the same language. Without AI, or if it fails, a text from the local corpus follows, with a note explaining it. In words mode, more words from the corpus always follow. Code mode does not use this option and keeps its line limit.

If you reach the end before the continuation, the text waits with “Generating more text…” and goes on as soon as it arrives. If the AI is slow or fails, the continuation comes from the local corpus.

## How the session ends

<kbd>Esc</kbd> or the “End” button closes the session and scores what you typed as a complete session: results, history, statistics, daily goal and progressive unlock. If you finish without typing anything, it is discarded and another text loads. “Repeat” brings back only the part you typed, as a normal text.

Unlimited texts do not race the ghost session, and the [metronome](/en/features/metronome)'s adaptive tempo does not change after them.

## Available lengths

| Option | Lengths |
| --- | --- |
| Characters | 100, 200, 300 (default), 450, 600, 800, 1000, 1500 and 2000 |
| No limit | “Unlimited”, in prose and words |
| Where to choose it | In the header, next to Prose / Words / Code, or in Settings → Practice → “Length” |

To continue the text, only the text already generated is sent to the AI, never what you type. More details in [optional AI](/en/features/ai).
