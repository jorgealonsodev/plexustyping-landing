## Eight layouts

The layout is chosen in Settings → Keyboard → “Layout”. It decides how the on-screen keyboard is drawn and which characters can appear in the texts: with English (US), for example, Spanish texts carry no ñ or accents.

| Layout | Shape | Dead keys |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Español (España), the default | ISO | ´ ¨ ^ and the grave accent |
| Español (Latinoamérica) | ISO | ´ ¨ ^ and the grave accent |
| English (US) | ANSI | none |
| Português (Brasil) | ABNT2 | ´ ^ ~ ¨ and the grave accent |
| Français (France), AZERTY | ISO | ^ ¨ |
| Deutsch (Deutschland), QWERTZ | ISO | ^ ´ and the grave accent |
| English (Dvorak) | ANSI | none |
| English (Colemak) | ANSI | none |

The app's layout has to match the operating system's, because keystrokes arrive from the system keyboard.

## Five text languages

In Settings → Practice → “Text language” you choose the language of the texts: Spanish (the default), English, Brazilian Portuguese, French or German. Each has its own offline local corpus, with the 5,000 most frequent words, everyday sentences, a diagnostic paragraph and up to 2,000 sentences from Mozilla Common Voice (CC0). With the [optional AI](/en/features/ai), texts are requested in that language.

## The interface in five languages

Since version 0.2.0, the menus, settings, notices and screen reader announcements come in Español, English, Português (Brasil), Français and Deutsch. On first start, the app follows the computer's language, and English when that one is not supported. Change it in Settings → Appearance → “Interface language”, separately from the text language: you can practice French texts with the interface in English. More in [text languages](/en/docs/keyboard-and-languages/text-languages).

## On-screen keyboard

<kbd>F2</kbd> shows or hides the on-screen keyboard, in ISO, ANSI or ABNT2 shape depending on the layout. It carries the Shift and AltGr labels, marks the dead keys, colors each key by finger, highlights the next key and its modifier and flashes green or red when you press. With the [adaptive engine](/en/features/adaptive-engine), it also shows the heatmap of your errors.
