With the metronome on, the pulse dot and the BPM shown next to the live statistics are also a button. Clicking it opens a small panel to adjust the metronome without leaving the text: tempo, sound, volume, accent and adaptive tempo, plus turning it off. The keys you press in the panel never reach the practice.

## Opening the panel

1. [Turn on the metronome](/en/docs/metronome/basics) with <kbd>F4</kbd>.
2. Click the pulse dot (with the BPM next to it) beside the live statistics.
3. Focus moves to the panel's first control.

## Controls

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Tempo − / + | Lowers or raises the tempo to the previous or next multiple of 5 BPM |
| Tempo (field) | Type the exact tempo; it applies on <kbd>Enter</kbd> or when you leave the field, within 30–300 BPM |
| Sound | “Click”, “Wood” or “Beep” |
| Volume | From 0 to 100% |
| Accent every | Every how many beats the accent sounds (0 = no accent) |
| Adaptive tempo | Turns the [adaptive tempo](/en/docs/metronome/adaptive-tempo) on or off |
| Turn off F4 | Turns the metronome off |
| Close | Closes the panel |

They are the same settings as Settings → Metronome: a change in one place shows in the other.

## With the keyboard

- <kbd>Tab</kbd> and <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Tab</kbd> move through the controls without leaving the panel.
- <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes the panel and returns focus to the pulse button, without restarting the text.
