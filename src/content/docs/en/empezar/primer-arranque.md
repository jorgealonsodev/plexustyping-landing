The first time you open PlexusTyping it offers a fixed diagnostic text, marked “Diagnostic text” above the text. It measures your errors and your pace for the first time. It repeats until you save your first session; from then on, every new text adapts to your weak spots.

## Before you type: check the layout

The app's keyboard layout must match the operating system's, because keystrokes arrive from the system keyboard. The default is Español (España).

1. Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>,</kbd> (or the gear button in the header) to open “Settings”.
2. In Keyboard → “Layout”, choose yours. Changing it loads a new text.
3. In Practice → “Text language”, choose the language of the texts.

The interface appears in the computer's language if it is English, Spanish, Portuguese, French or German, and otherwise in English. To change it, go to “Appearance” → “Interface language”.

The eight layouts are in [keyboard layouts](/en/docs/keyboard-and-languages/layouts) and the five languages in [text languages](/en/docs/keyboard-and-languages/text-languages).

## Your first text

1. Start typing: the clock starts with the first key.
2. Each character is marked right or wrong as you type. The live statistics bar shows WPM, keystrokes per minute, accuracy and time.
3. At the end, the results screen shows net and gross WPM, accuracy, time, errors and the missed characters.
4. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> (“Next text”) for the first adaptive text, or “Repeat” to type the same one again.

While you type, <kbd>Esc</kbd> restarts the text and <kbd>F5</kbd> discards it and loads a new one. Accents and dead keys work as in any program: `´` followed by `a` gives `á`.

## The on-screen keyboard

Below the text you see a keyboard drawn with your layout that highlights the next key. <kbd>F2</kbd> shows or hides it. More in [on-screen keyboard and flashes](/en/docs/keyboard-and-languages/on-screen-keyboard-and-flashes).

## If “Not saving: temporary mode” appears

“Not saving: temporary mode” means the app could not open its local database. It works the same, but with a temporary in-memory store: this run's sessions are lost when you close it.
