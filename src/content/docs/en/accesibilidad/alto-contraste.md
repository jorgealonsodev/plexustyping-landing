PlexusTyping includes two high-contrast themes: “Alto contraste oscuro” (high contrast dark), with white text on black, and “Alto contraste claro” (high contrast light), with black text on white. Their colors reach 7:1 for text and 4.5:1 for interface components. The other 57 bundled themes meet WCAG AA. Choose them in Settings → Appearance → “Tema”.

## The two themes

| Theme | Background and text | State and keyboard colors |
|---|---|---|
| Alto contraste oscuro (dark) | White text on black | Light, saturated ANSI colors |
| Alto contraste claro (light) | Black text on white | Dark, saturated ANSI colors |

| Minimum contrast | Value |
|---|---|
| Text | 7:1 (WCAG AAA) |
| Interface components | 4.5:1 |

## Pending text is not dimmed

With these two themes, the text still to type is never dimmed, even with “Atenuar texto pendiente” (dim pending text) on. See [dimmed pending text](/en/docs/personalization/dimmed-pending-text).

## Combining with other settings

- [Typography](/en/docs/personalization/typography): font size up to 200 % and line height up to 2.4.
- [Color-blind mode](/en/docs/accessibility/color-blind-mode): flashes in blue and orange.
- [Fonts](/en/docs/personalization/fonts): the “Legibilidad” (legibility) group.
