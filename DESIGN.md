# Design System: PlexusTyping website

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The app, opened on the web"**

The website wears the PlexusTyping desktop app's own skin, so what a visitor sees is what they download. The ground, surfaces, text and accent colours are the app's tokens, generated by the app's own `typingColors()` from its bundled Catppuccin Mocha (dark) and Catppuccin Latte (light) palettes and copied into `src/styles/tokens.css`; the prose and UI use the app's system sans stack; practice text, keys and numbers use JetBrains Mono, the app's default practice font. The site adds only what a website needs around the app: a header, a footer, reading layouts, and the window frame that holds each simulation.

Density is calm and generous on the Persuade surfaces (home, features, download): centred hero, one idea per section, a live simulation as the argument. The Read surfaces (documentation, FAQ, release notes, privacy, licences) are quieter and denser: a reading column of 62ch, a sidebar and an on-page table of contents, hairline tables. Decoration the app itself would not have is refused: no gradients on text, no glass, no illustrations, no invented screenshots. The only atmospheric touch is a soft accent glow behind the hero window, as in the chosen sketch (`.impeccable/mocks/decision/canon.html`).

The simulations (S1–S10) are not part of this system: they are the app, rebuilt from the app repository's own components, CSS and data (`src/sim/`). They follow the app's design, not this file.

**Key Characteristics:**
- App tokens, never redrawn: every colour comes from `tokens.css` (regenerated from the app, not edited by hand) or a `color-mix()` of those tokens.
- Two themes that follow the system (`prefers-color-scheme`), with a footer toggle that stores the choice; dark is the default identity.
- Hairline borders and tonal surfaces instead of shadows; one deep shadow under app windows.
- System sans for words, JetBrains Mono for anything typed, counted or pressed.
- Motion lives inside the simulations; the site itself only fades between pages.

## Colors

The palette is the app's: Catppuccin Mocha by night and Latte by day, one blue accent, warm status colours, and the binding logo's fixed colours.

### Primary
- **Mocha Blue** (`accent-mocha`): primary buttons, focus rings, the second line of the hero headline, the active docs sidebar rail, outlines of the detected download choice. In dark themes it is also the link colour (7.1–7.8:1 on the site's surfaces).
- **Latte Blue** (`accent-latte`): the same roles in the light theme. It carries white button text at 4.9:1 and large headings at 4.3:1, but only 3.9–4.3:1 as small text, so links use Latte Link.
- **Latte Link** (`link-latte`): link text in the light theme, the accent mixed 85 % with black (`--link` in `global.css`), 5.0–5.6:1 on every light surface.

### Secondary
- **Next-key Yellow** (`warning-mocha` / `warning-latte`): the tint of «Próxima versión» / «Next version» badges (20 % mix), and the next key inside simulations.
- **Error Pink** (`danger-*`) and **Success Green** (`success-*`): only inside simulations and status text, as the app uses them.

### Neutral
- **Ground** (`ground-*`): page background and the app's own window background.
- **Surface** (`surface-*`): app windows, the footer, full-width bands on the home, quoted app dialogs.
- **Surface 2** (`surface-2-*`): window title bars, note panels, table heads, inline code.
- **Hairline** (`hairline-*`): every border, divider, table rule and ghost-button outline.
- **Text** (`text-*`) and **Muted** (`muted-*`): body text and secondary text (5.1:1 muted on the Latte ground, 5.5:1 on the Mocha ground).

### Named Rules
**The App Token Rule.** A colour on this site is an app token or a `color-mix()` of app tokens. `tokens.css` is regenerated from the app, never edited by hand; site-only aliases (`--link`, `--sans`, `--mono`, radii, gutter, measure) live in `global.css`.

**The Link Contrast Rule.** Small text in the accent uses `var(--link)`, not `var(--accent)`. The raw accent is for fills, outlines, focus rings and text of 24px and larger.

**The Binding Logo Rule.** The keycap logo and wordmark are used as shipped (`src/assets/logo.svg`, `logo-wordmark.svg`); their gradient and homing-bump colours are fixed and appear nowhere else.

## Typography

**Display Font:** system-ui (the app's UI stack; no web font for prose)
**Body Font:** system-ui
**Label/Mono Font:** JetBrains Mono 400/700, self-hosted WOFF2, latin + latin-ext (`src/styles/fonts.css`)

**Character:** A heavy, tightly tracked system sans gives headlines the weight of a native app; JetBrains Mono marks anything the reader would type or count, exactly as in the app.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (800, `clamp(2.25rem, 1.2rem + 4.4vw, 4rem)`, 1.05, -0.03em): the home hero headline only; its second line in the accent.
- **Headline** (800, `clamp(1.875rem, 1.2rem + 2.6vw, 3rem)`, 1.1, -0.025em): home section headings; feature page titles run a step larger (`clamp(2.25rem, 1.4rem + 3.5vw, 3.75rem)`).
- **Title** (700, `clamp(2rem, 1.4rem + 2.2vw, 2.875rem)`, -0.02em): documentation and prose page titles.
- **Body** (400, 1.0625rem, 1.7): documentation and prose; home and feature copy run at `clamp(1.0625rem, 1rem + 0.3vw, 1.1875rem)` in the muted colour. Measure 62ch (about 75 characters).
- **Lead** (400, `clamp(1.0625rem, 0.95rem + 0.5vw, 1.125rem)`, 1.6): the hero's 40–60 word answer, centred at 46rem; it stays at 1.125rem so the four-line lead and both buttons leave the app window's top edge in the first desktop viewport.
- **Label** (600, 0.8125–0.875rem): meta lines (section · updated · app version), window titles, captions (0.9375rem, muted), the uppercase docs sidebar groups (700, 0.8125rem, +0.04em).
- **Mono** (JetBrains Mono, 0.875–0.9em of its context): inline code, keycaps (`kbd`), checksums, numbers.

### Named Rules
**The Typed-Things Rule.** Monospace means "you type or count this": code, keys, file names, checksums, statistics. Never as a costume for headings or labels.

**The No-Eyebrow Rule.** No kicker above a heading. A documentation page names its section first in the meta line under the title, not above it.

## Layout

A single centred column with a fluid gutter (`clamp(1rem, 4vw, 3.5rem)`). Persuade content tops out at 65rem (the app-window width) and copy at 44rem; prose pages at 56rem with a 62ch reading measure; the documentation grid widens to 88rem with a left sidebar (search + sections) from 56rem and a right table of contents from 80rem. Below 56rem the docs sidebar follows the article in the HTML, so the article paints first without layout shift.

Home sections are separated by `clamp(4rem, 10vw, 7.5rem)`; full-width bands use Surface with hairlines above and below. Grids that hold prose use one `minmax(0, 1fr)` track so a wide table or code block scrolls inside its own box; on phones (≤ 40rem) prose tables become horizontally scrollable blocks and inline code may break anywhere. Every page fits 390px without sideways scrolling (checked across all 161 built pages).

Breakpoints in use: 30rem (phone: full-width hero buttons, icon-only play buttons), 40rem (feature-page window bars drop their dots, prose tables scroll), 48rem (below it the header links and the download button move into the phone drawer; footer preferences align right), 56rem and 80rem (docs columns). Simulations size themselves with container queries on the app window (44rem, 24rem), copied from the app's own window breakpoints.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat by default, depth by tone: Ground → Surface → Surface 2, each step separated by a 1px hairline. The one shadow is the app window's (`0 1.875rem 5rem`, 12 % black in light, 45 % in dark via `light-dark()`), which lifts the simulations off the page the way a desktop window sits above its wallpaper. The hero adds a soft radial accent glow (13 % accent) behind its window, clamped to the viewport width.

### Named Rules
**The One Window Shadow Rule.** Only app windows cast a shadow. Cards, notes, tables and buttons are flat with hairlines.

## Shapes

Gently rounded, from the app: 0.375rem for tags and the app's own buttons, 0.625rem (`--radius`) for controls, notes and quoted dialogs, 0.75rem for large buttons, 1rem (`--radius-lg`) for app windows and download choices, full pills for badges. Borders are 1px hairlines; the only thicker line is the 2px rail of the docs sidebar, which turns accent on the current page. Window title bars carry three neutral dots (hidden below 40rem on feature pages) and a muted title.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** gently rounded (0.625rem; 0.75rem for the large hero and download buttons).
- **Primary:** accent fill, on-accent text, bold, 2.75rem tall (3.5rem large, 1.25rem text).
- **Hover / Focus:** fill mixes 14 % toward the text colour over 160ms (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`); focus is the app's 2px accent outline at 2px offset.
- **Ghost:** transparent with a hairline; hover raises the hairline to muted and fills with Surface. The download page turns the button of the system not detected into a ghost.

### Badges
- **Next version:** a pill tinted 20 % with Next-key Yellow, text colour, bold 0.75–0.8125rem: «Próxima versión 0.1.1» / «Next version 0.1.1». Every claim about 0.1.1 or planned work carries it.
- **Detected system:** a pill tinted 12 % with the accent, its text in the link blue, «Tu sistema» / «Your system», on the download choice that matches the visitor's OS.

### App window (signature)
The frame every simulation lives in: Surface panel, 1rem radius, hairline, the One Window Shadow; a 2.375rem Surface 2 title bar with three dots and a muted title («PlexusTyping · demostración» / «PlexusTyping · demo»). Website controls (hint text, play/pause, the next-version tag) sit on the bar's right, outside the app; on phones the play button keeps only its icon and accessible name. A muted caption under the window, at most 62ch, says the data is a demonstration.

### Notes and quoted dialogs
Surface 2 (notes) or Surface (quoted app dialogs in documentation) panels with a hairline and 0.625rem radius. No coloured side bars.

### Tables
Full width, hairline row rules, muted 600-weight headers (Surface 2 head on feature pages), 0.9375rem text, top-aligned cells.

### Navigation
- **Header:** logo + wordmark text, three muted links (current page in the text colour), primary «Descargar» / «Download» button on the right; the header keeps its place across page transitions (`view-transition-name: site-header`).
- **Phone drawer (below 48rem):** the three links leave the header row; a 2.75rem ghost button with a three-line icon («Abrir menú» / «Open menu») sits at the right end of the header row (the «Descargar» button leaves the header and moves into the drawer, under the links) and opens a `<nav>` («Menú» / «Menu») that slides in from the right: full height, `min(20rem, 85vw)` wide, Surface with a hairline on its left edge, over a black backdrop (40 % light, 60 % dark). It is a native `popover` opened by `popovertarget`, so it needs no JavaScript; Esc or a tap on the backdrop closes it, and so does the ghost close button («Cerrar menú» / «Close menu») placed where the open button was. Links are 1.125rem, 3rem tall, muted on the docs sidebar's 2px hairline rail; the current section is bold, text-coloured, on an accent rail. The slide (280ms, `@starting-style` + transform) and the backdrop fade are off under reduced motion. Browsers without popover support keep the old second row of links.
- **Docs sidebar:** a search trigger, then uppercase muted section groups and their pages on a 2px hairline rail; the current page is bold, text-coloured, with an accent rail.
- **Footer:** Surface band with the wordmark, secondary links, the language switch and the theme toggle (rendered only with JavaScript; without it the page follows the system).

### Feature list
Rows rather than cards: each feature is a hairline-separated row with its name, an optional badge, a muted summary and an arrow; the whole row is the link target and takes the focus ring.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** take every colour from `tokens.css` or a `color-mix()` of it, and regenerate `tokens.css` from the app when the app changes.
- **Do** use `var(--link)` for small accent-coloured text, so links pass 4.5:1 in Latte.
- **Do** build simulations from the app repository's components and CSS (`src/sim/`), keep its fonts, cursor and keyboard, and label their data as a demonstration.
- **Do** quote the app's Spanish labels in English pages in curly quotes with an English gloss: “Ajustes” (settings). The build marks them `lang="es"` (`src/middleware.ts`).
- **Do** use US spelling in English copy and English simulation strings; the English practice text is the app's corpus, copied verbatim.
- **Do** keep motion inside the simulations and behind `prefers-reduced-motion`: under reduced motion nothing transitions or animates (global rule), page View Transitions are off, and simulations wait for the visitor to press play.
- **Do** keep pages complete without JavaScript: the theme toggle, search and simulation controls only enhance.

### Don't:
- **Don't** edit `tokens.css` by hand or invent colours outside it.
- **Don't** redraw the app by eye, restyle a simulation to match the site, or show invented screenshots, testimonials or numbers.
- **Don't** put a kicker or eyebrow above a heading.
- **Don't** use coloured side bars thicker than 1px on notes, quotes or cards, gradient text, glass, or decorative shadows.
- **Don't** use monospace for headings or labels that are not typed or counted.
- **Don't** load third-party fonts, scripts or images; JetBrains Mono is self-hosted and the prose uses the system sans.
- **Don't** alter the logo, its colours or its proportions.
